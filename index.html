01 / The ECU

NX-ECU Minimum

The Minimum Version of our standalone ECU delivers deterministic engine control powered by 100% custom firmware executing over 600,000 loops per second. Featuring 16x16 fuel and ignition maps, closed-loop idle, and >50Hz PC live data and logging.

  • Firmware100% Custom (>600,000 loops/sec)
  • Outputs2x Ignition, 2x Injection out
  • Tables & Idle16x16 Maps, Closed-loop idle
  • Analog & Sensors0-5V Analog, Ext. Wideband & Knock
  • PC Telemetry>50Hz PC Live Data & Logging
02 / Race hardware

Custom PCB & Race Hardware

Engineered around a custom noise-isolated PCB layout with protected power stages and clean 0-5V analog signal conditioning for external wideband O2 and knock controllers. Packed with dedicated motorsport strategies and auxiliary outputs straight out of the box.

  • PCB & InputsNoise-isolated PCB, 0-5V Analog I/O
  • Launch & BoostLaunch Control & Antilag (ALS)
  • ShiftingQuick Shifter & Shift Light
  • TractionTime-Based Traction Control
  • Aux OutputsNOS Injection Out, Thermo Fan, Fuel pump, Idle valve, Shift Light, Light

04 / Free tool

Base Map Generator

Generate 16x16 base VE, ignition and AFR maps from your engine parameters, edit them in 2D or inspect them in 3D, then export straight to TunerStudio.

Drag or shift-click to select. Double-click a cell to type a value. Keys + and - nudge by 0.5, Ctrl+I interpolates.
